# Fictional media agency (seller side)

> Entirely fictional. It describes no real company, nor its offers or prices. It gives the assistant a seller and a catalogue of offers to propose.

**Agence Hexamédia** sells advertising space and partnerships on four editorial sites:

| Site | Theme | Target brand sector |
|---|---|---|
| Saveurs&Co | cooking, recipes, products | food |
| Vitalité Mag | health, well-being, nutrition | health |
| Cépages Magazine | wine, beer, spirits, pairings | alcohol |
| Pattes & Compagnie | pets | petfood |

**Four standard offers** (generic, no price):
1. **Display and video advertising**: banners and videos targeted by editorial theme.
2. **Branded content**: sponsored article or recipe, written with the brand.
3. **Influence partnership**: operation with the site's content creators.
4. **Special operation**: contest, event or seasonal highlight.

Common rule: the assistant proposes the offer best suited to the brand from its facts, without promising quantified results or a price.
